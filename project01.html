Project 1: Getting to Know You

I built an amusement park ride, specifically one from my local amusement park. The ride works with the carts going around in a circle and off a jump one time per rotation. It's called the kangaroo and growing up it was one of my favorite rides and although I have steadily outgrown it, I still fondly remember the countless times I dragged my parents to ride it with me. How the lego contraption works is there is a small hand crank that when you spin, spins the larger contraption and on one side there is a slope piece that pushes each of the “carts” up making a little jump.
