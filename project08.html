Project 8: Robot Arm

For this project, I designed, built and programmed a robotic arm that picks up a ping-pong ball at one end of its base and delivers it to the other end. The base uses purple 1x9 beams to keep a row of Technic holes centered, so it can connect end-to-end with my classmates’ arms and pass the ball from one arm to the next. The video shows my arm working on its own, then working with another arm to hand off the ball.
