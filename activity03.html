Activity 3: Paper Airplane

The AI preformed fairly well given this exact prompt: I do not know how to make a paper airplane at all. could you give me exact, detailed, no room for error, step by step instructions to help me make a paper air plane. Only give me one step at a time and give me the next step when i ask for it. It gave me step by step instructions along with pixel drawing of what my piece of paper should look like at each step. I followed the instructions exactly as the AI provided and made a paper airplane. I then tested it and it flew fairly well. .
