Activity 1: Drink Coaster

The design activity went very smoothly using Gen AI. We asked copilot to come up with interview questions to design the most unique and specific mug coaster for a very specific fiction/nonfictional character in the history of existence. We then used those questions to interview copilot identifying as Alexander the Great and used the answers to enter into ChatGPT to generate detailed images of the mugs. I learned that copilot cannot generate images and it was interesting to see how specific the questions were.
